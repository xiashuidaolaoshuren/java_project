# Fixed breaks and commitments are two aggregates, one scheduler concept

A fixed break is a recurring preference wholly inside its work window; a commitment is an independently dated fact whose date may be edited. They are persisted separately and normalized by stage 1 into non-overlapping unavailable segments with precedence `COMMITMENT > FIXED_BREAK > BUFFER`, subtracting overlaps once. Same-date commitments may not overlap because FocusFlow models one feasible personal day and has no tentative or parallel-calendar semantics. Fixed and cadence breaks reset accumulated focus; commitments do not count as rest. A single nullable-date entity would mix lifecycles, while transient commitments on generate would vanish during 1.3.0 re-planning.
